The Seahorse

Barnard 150 (LDN 1082), in Cepheus  ·  HaLRGB — about 20 hours over seven nights, October–November 2026

Barnard 150 (LDN 1082)  ·  HaLRGB  ·  ©2026 Peter F. Wingard  ·  @see_theShow

A dark cloud in silhouette

Dark nebula in Cepheus, about 1,200 light-years away: a dense cloud of dust and cold gas seen in silhouette against the background stars and the fainter dust around it. The densest knots are catalogued as LDN 1082 A, B and C. Hydrogen emission glows at the right edge of the field.

Colour: RGB with SPCC, Ha added to the red channel only where it exceeds its median.

Acquisition

Telescope
Askar 80 at 495 mm
Camera
ZWO ASI2600MM Pro, ZWO EFW, ZWO EAF — guided (PHD2), NINA
Filters
Antlia V-Pro L / R / G / B, Chroma 3 nm Ha
Settings
Gain 100, offset 50, sensor −10C, bin 1
Nights
2026-10-11 — R / G / B 36 × 60s each
2026-10-12 — L 123 × 90s
2026-10-13 — L 85 × 90s, G 11 × 60s
2026-10-14 — L 184 × 90s, G 13 × 60s
2026-10-15 — L 194 × 90s
2026-10-21 — Ha 61 × 180s
2026-11-02 — R 52 × 60s, G 31 × 60s, B 31 × 60s
Stacked
Kept after a sub-by-sub review for haze, cloud, trees and star shape: L 556 of 586 (13h54m), R 84 of 88 (1h24m), G 80 of 91 (1h20m), B 66 of 67 (1h06m), Ha 47 of 61 (2h21m) = about 20h05m total
Location
Camden, South Carolina — Bortle 4
Calibration
Flats + flat-darks per night, darks and bias at −10C from the library
Processing
Siril: calibration, registration to one grid, drizzle, one master per filter. PixInsight: MultiscaleGradientCorrection, ImageSolver + SPCC, StarXTerminator, MultiscaleAdaptiveStretch with a background reference, Ha into red with PixelMath, layered curves on the luminance for depth, LRGB combination, NoiseXTerminator, a masked hue curve on the hydrogen wall. Stars from the RGB, screened back in at 0.525.

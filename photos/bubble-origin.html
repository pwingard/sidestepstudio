The Bubble Nebula

NGC 7635, in Cassiopeia  ·  Celestron Origin — 1 h 55 m in one night, October 15, 2026

The Bubble Nebula (NGC 7635)  ·  Celestron Origin, UV/IR-cut  ·  ©2026 Peter F. Wingard  ·  @see_theShow

One star, one bubble

The Bubble Nebula, NGC 7635 in Cassiopeia: a shell of gas 7 light-years across blown by one huge star, about 8,000 light-years away. I’ve avoided faint nebulae like this on the Celestron Origin because of walking noise but not any longer.

Acquisition

Telescope
Celestron Origin — RASA 6″, 335 mm, f/2.2
Camera
Origin built-in colour camera — ISO 500
Filter
UV/IR-cut
Integration
231 × 30s = 1 h 55 m
Dates
October 15, 2026 (one night)
Location
My backyard site at The Star Farm in Camden, South Carolina — Bortle 4.5
Processing
Stacked in Siril, processed in PixInsight
